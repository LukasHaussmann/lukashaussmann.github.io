PBR Estimation with Diffusion-generated Relightings

Material estimation from a single photo: an image model (Flux, GPT-Image-2) relights the scene, then inverse rendering with a Cook-Torrance renderer recovers albedo, roughness and metallic that explain every relighting.

with Felix Laarmann and Matthias Nießner · TUM Visual Computing & AI Lab

Instant Surface Reconstruction

Dense initialization for 2D Gaussian Splatting: triangulated feature matches, estimated normals and depth filtering give well-placed surfels from iteration zero. Matches fully trained 2DGS on DTU (0.74 mm Chamfer) in 2 minutes instead of 15.

with Jon Ayo Gieß · supervised by Lukas Höllein · TUM Visual Computing & AI Lab
